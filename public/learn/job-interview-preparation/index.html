How to prepare for a job interview

By the VENUE team · November 2026

An interview is not a test designed to make you fail. It is a chance for the employer to know you and for you to decide whether the company suits you. Good preparation reduces nerves.

Before the interview

  • Research the company: what they do, who their customers are, and their recent news.
  • Reread the job advert: match the skills they ask for with the skills you have.
  • Prepare real examples: things you did in class, on projects or while volunteering.
  • Know the place and time: arrive 15 minutes early, or test the meeting link beforehand for online interviews.

Common questions

  1. “Tell us about yourself.” Mention where you are in your studies, one or two main skills, and why this role interests you. One minute is enough.
  2. “Why do you want this job?” Connect your goals to the company’s work.
  3. “What is your weakness?” Name a real weakness and what you are doing to improve it.
  4. “Tell us about a challenge you overcame.” Use this structure: situation, what you did, result.
  5. “Do you have questions for us?” Have two ready, for example about training or how success is measured in the role.

On the day

  • Dress neatly and in line with the workplace.
  • Listen to the whole question before answering. It is fine to take a few seconds to think.
  • Be honest. If you do not know something, say so and explain how you would find out.
  • Thank the interviewer at the end, and send a short thank-you message the next day.
