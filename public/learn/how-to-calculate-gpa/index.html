How to calculate your GPA, step by step

By the VENUE team · November 2026

GPA (Grade Point Average) is the weighted average of your course grades. Universities use it to decide whether you pass, qualify for awards, or move on to the next year. Knowing how to calculate it yourself tells you where you stand before results are released.

The GPA formula

GPA = (total weighted points for all courses) ÷ (total credits for all courses).

The weighted points for one course are the grade points of your grade × the credits of that course.

Steps

  1. List all courses for the semester with their credits.
  2. Write down the grade you got in each course (A, B+, B, C and so on).
  3. Convert each grade to grade points using your university’s scale.
  4. Multiply the grade points by the course credits.
  5. Add up the results, then divide by the total credits.

Worked example

Suppose the scale is A = 5, B+ = 4, B = 3, C = 2. Your own scale may differ, so check your programme’s examination regulations.

Total credits = 3 + 3 + 2 + 4 = 12. Total weighted points = 12 + 15 + 6 + 8 = 41. GPA = 41 ÷ 12 = 3.42.

Useful tips

  • A course with more credits affects your GPA more than one with fewer credits.
  • If your university lets you retake courses, check how the new grade is counted.
  • For a cumulative GPA, add up the points and credits across all semesters. Do not average the semester GPAs.

The official grading scale and GPA rules are those in your university’s regulations. Use this article as a learning guide and confirm the details with your university.
