First year at university: 10 things to know

By the VENUE team · November 2026

Starting university is a big change. Nobody supervises you every day, and organising yourself is your responsibility. Here are ten things that can help you start well.

  1. Know your courses early. Check the semester’s courses, their credits and the lecturer for each.
  2. Read the examination regulations. Know the pass marks, how supplementary exams work, and what happens if you miss an exam.
  3. Attend lectures regularly. Early lectures give a course overview and hints about the exams.
  4. Start studying early. Do not wait for exam week. Study a little every day.
  5. Form a study group. Two to four students who get along can help each other with hard topics.
  6. Use the library and university systems. Learn the student information system, your university email and the library.
  7. Join one or two clubs. They help you make friends and build skills outside class.
  8. Manage money carefully. Make a simple budget from the start.
  9. Look after your health. Eat well, sleep enough and do light exercise. If you feel stressed, talk to someone you trust or the university counselling office.
  10. Ask for help early. Lecturers, academic advisers and senior students can help if you ask.

The first year is about learning not only your courses but also how to live and manage yourself. Start slowly, and you will keep improving.
