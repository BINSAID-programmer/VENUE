8 proven ways to study for exams

By the VENUE team · November 2026

Many students study for hours but remember little. The problem is often not effort but method. Here are eight simple techniques you can start using today.

1. Test yourself instead of rereading

Closing the book and trying to recall what you just studied builds memory better than reading the same page three times. Use past papers or write your own questions.

2. Study in short blocks

Study for 25 to 40 minutes, then rest for 5 to 10. Attention drops when you study for long stretches without a break.

3. Spread your studying out

Studying a topic for one hour on four different days works better than four hours the night before the exam.

4. Teach someone else

Explain the topic to a friend in plain words. Where you get stuck shows you what you have not understood yet.

5. Practise past papers

They show the style of questions and the topics that repeat. Try them under real exam time.

6. Write a one-page summary

For each course, put formulas, definitions and key arguments on one page. You will review it quickly on the last day.

7. Get enough sleep

Sleep is when your brain stores what you learned. Staying up all night before an exam usually hurts more than it helps.

8. Remove distractions

Put your phone away or turn off notifications. Thirty uninterrupted minutes are worth more than two hours with constant messages.

Pick two or three of these and start this week. Once they become habits, the results will show.
