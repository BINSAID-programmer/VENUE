How to manage money as a university student

By the VENUE team · November 2026

Many students receive their money in one lump sum and then run out before the semester ends. A simple budget can help you avoid that.

Step 1: Know your income

Add up all the money you expect for the semester: student loan, family support, part-time work and anything else.

Step 2: List your must-have expenses

These include accommodation, food, transport, phone and internet, study materials and small fees.

Step 3: Divide by week

Divide the semester’s money by the number of weeks. That gives you what you can spend each week without running out. This is safer than spending big at the start.

Step 4: Keep an emergency fund

Set aside a small amount each month, even a very small one, for emergencies such as medical costs or a broken phone.

Tips that help

  • Write down your spending for two weeks. You will be surprised where the money goes.
  • Cook or buy food together with friends if that lowers the cost.
  • Buy longer-lasting internet bundles if they are cheaper.
  • Avoid quick loans with high interest.
  • Use mobile money carefully and protect your PIN.

Remember, a budget changes. Review it every week and adjust it.
