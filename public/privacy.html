VENUE Privacy Policy

Sera ya Faragha ya VENUE (Your Academic Space) · Last updated: 24 October 2026

VENUE is a study app for university students. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have. By using VENUE you agree to it.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number (optional) and profile photo (optional), and your sign-in method (email/password or Google).
  • Academic details: country, university, college, department, programme, year of study, semester and registration number.
  • Content you create: community posts, comments, chat messages, study plans, tasks, notes and results you save.
  • AI Tutor questions: text or photos you send to the AI Tutor.
  • Usage and device information: basic usage events (such as which features are used), online/last-active status in Community, and technical logs needed to keep the app secure and working.

2. How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and show your correct courses and materials.
  • To provide Community, chat, announcements, the planner and the AI Tutor.
  • To improve the app, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • To contact you about your account or support requests.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Who processes your data

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore database, Storage) stores account data and content.
  • Google Gemini processes AI Tutor questions to generate answers.
  • Hosting providers (such as Vercel and our server host) deliver the app and run its server.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms and security measures.

4. Who can see your information

Your academic profile is visible to authorised VENUE administrators. In Community, your name, photo and posts are visible to other students according to the community you post in. Private chat messages are visible to the people in that chat. You can hide your online status in Community settings.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Edit: change your name, phone, photo, year/semester, and correct your university or programme from Profile → Edit.
  • Download: export your data from Profile → Edit → “Pakua data yangu”.
  • Delete: permanently delete your account and data from Profile → Edit → “Futa akaunti yangu”. Some records may remain briefly in backups or security logs before being removed.

6. Data retention and security

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account we remove your profile and personal data. We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), access rules and authentication to protect data, but no online service is perfectly secure.

7. Children

VENUE is intended for university students. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be communicated in the app.
