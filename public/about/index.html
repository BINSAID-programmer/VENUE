About VENUE

VENUE (“Your Academic Space”) is an app for university students. It was created to help students find materials for their courses, study with the help of an AI Tutor, track their GPA, and connect with fellow students in an organised way.

What we offer

  • Course materials shared by verified lecturers and students, and reviewed before they are published.
  • An AI Tutor to help you understand topics and answer study questions.
  • A GPA tracker to follow your results.
  • A community of students, with scholarship information and career opportunities.

Trust and copyright

All materials go through a verification process. Lecturers are verified before they can upload, and student files are reviewed by the VENUE team before other students can see them. When we receive a copyright complaint, we remove the content concerned.
